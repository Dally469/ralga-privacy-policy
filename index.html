Privacy Policy

RALGA Mobile Application

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Publisher: Rwanda Association of Local Government Authorities (RALGA)

No account required. The RALGA App is free to use and does not require users to create an account to access its content.

1. Introduction

The Rwanda Association of Local Government Authorities (RALGA) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use the RALGA mobile application (“the App”).

The App is published by RALGA, a non-governmental association. It is not an official government application and does not represent or operate on behalf of any government entity.

2. Purpose of the App

RALGA provides convenient access to laws, regulations, legal documents, and other information relevant to local government in Rwanda. Topics include:

  • Local government authorities
  • Land and land-related matters
  • Persons and communities
  • Cities and districts
  • Laws and regulations
  • Other legal resources relevant to local government in Rwanda

3. Account and Personal Information

The App does not require account registration or login to use its core content. You do not need to provide your name, phone number, or email address to read the legal resources available in the App.

If you contact RALGA directly by email, RALGA will receive the information you choose to include in your message and may use it to respond.

4. Technical Information

Depending on the App's configuration and the services it uses, technical information such as device details, crash reports, diagnostics, or basic usage information may be processed to maintain security, troubleshoot issues, and improve reliability. The exact information depends on the technologies enabled in the published App.

Do not interpret this section as a statement that any particular analytics or tracking service is enabled. RALGA should update this policy if the App's actual data practices change.

5. How Information May Be Used

Information that is processed may be used to:

  • Operate, maintain, and secure the App
  • Diagnose errors and improve performance
  • Respond to messages sent to RALGA
  • Meet applicable legal obligations

6. Sharing of Information

RALGA does not sell personal information. Information may be shared when reasonably necessary with service providers supporting the App, when required by law, or when needed to protect the security and rights of users or RALGA. Any such sharing should be limited to the relevant purpose and handled with appropriate safeguards.

7. Official Sources and External Links

Users can verify laws and government information through these official resources:

  • Rwanda Laws Portal – Ministry of Justice
  • Ministry of Local Government (MINALOC)

External websites operate under their own terms and privacy policies. RALGA does not control their privacy practices.

8. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information processed through the App against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for its purpose or as required by applicable law.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that RALGA holds about you, or raise concerns about its processing. To make a request, contact RALGA using the email below.

10. Children's Privacy

The App provides legal and educational information and does not require an account to access its core content. RALGA does not intend to collect personal information from children unnecessarily. If you believe personal information has been submitted inappropriately, please contact RALGA.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App, its services, applicable law, or data practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

12. Legal Disclaimer

The App is provided for general informational and educational purposes only. It is not an official government application and is not operated by a government ministry or agency as an official service.

The information in the App does not replace official government publications, original legal documents, or professional legal advice. Although reasonable efforts may be made to provide useful information, RALGA does not guarantee that all content is complete, current, or free from errors. Verify laws and regulations using official sources before relying on them for legal or official purposes. RALGA does not provide legal advice through this App.
